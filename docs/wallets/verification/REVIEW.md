# Adversarial review: triage

Status: the triage of the 43 findings of the adversarial review of `docs/wallets/` and `docs/wallets.md`, dated
2026-11-23 (principle 11). Each finding is **fixed**, **partly fixed** (what remains is said, and why) or
**rejected** (with the reason). Nothing was dismissed without a reason. The fixes were checked by rerunning
`../model/check_transitions.py`, TLC (`../model/README.md`, Results), `check-scenarios.mjs --coverage`,
`../features/check_features.py`, `mutations.mjs` and `../uml/render.sh`.

## Gaps left open by this triage

- OD-11 is a precondition, not a design (finding 1).
- No trace validation between the table's guards and the TLA+ actions (finding 19).
- No check of three fault classes racing at once (finding 25).
- The power-loss runner for C21 is not chosen or built (finding 26, spike I12).
- The model state `Queued` and the TLA+ identifier `Intents` keep their names (finding 39).
- TLC runs stopped before exhausting their state space: `PaymentLane_restore`, `X402_safety`, `X402_nondurable`,
  `PaymentLane_live3` and `X402_live` (model/README.md, Results).
- `min-release-age-exclude` with a scope pattern, the anvil pin and its skip rule were written, not run.
